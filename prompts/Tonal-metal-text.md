═══ THE LETTERING STYLE ═══
Extreme metal band logo lettering, hand-drawn style:
- Thick angular letterforms with high contrast between heavy stems and thin strokes
- Sharp pointed THORN SPIKES growing outward from the tops and bases of the stems,
  tapering to fine points
- On the FIRST and LAST letter of each line, the spikes extend further outward and
  symmetrically, like wings, framing the whole logo
- A few short DRIPS hanging from the bases of some letters
- Letters slightly overlapping, with spikes crossing over neighboring letters
- Irregular baseline — letters vary slightly in height and tilt
- Despite the thorns, the words MUST remain clearly READABLE

═══ EMBROIDERY RENDERING ═══
Every stroke is a raised satin-stitch column, domed and slightly glossy, sitting
visibly above the fabric surface. Strong directional sheen — stitch rows run ACROSS
each stroke, so some rows catch light brightly while neighboring rows stay dark purely
from thread direction. Individual thread strands visible along the edges. The thorn
spikes are solid satin columns, not hairlines — each spike thick enough to catch light.
The embroidery sits proud of the fabric and casts a soft contact shadow along its
right side. Gentle fabric puckering right at the stitch edges, tiny needle holes along
the borders. Sewn into the garment, never pressed into it, never printed.
