Convert the attached 2D flat artwork into a PRODUCTION-READY MACHINE EMBROIDERY design, then present it stitched onto a garment mockup.

STEP 1 — DIGITIZE INTO COLOR BLOCKS:
- Reduce the entire artwork to a MAXIMUM OF 5 SOLID THREAD COLORS. No gradients, no blends, no halftones, no anti-aliased soft edges — every area becomes one flat block of a single thread color with a hard boundary.
- Merge small tonal variations into the nearest block. Simplify fussy micro-detail that is smaller than 1.5mm and would be unstitchable.
- Thicken every thin line to a minimum 1.5mm satin-stitch column so it can be physically stitched; delete hairlines that are too fine.
- Every shape must be CLOSED and self-contained, sitting edge-to-edge with its neighbor block (butt-registered), never overlapping into mush.
- Total design size approximately 20cm wide — proportions and silhouette of the original artwork must remain recognizable and unchanged.

STEP 2 — ASSIGN STITCH TYPES PER BLOCK:
- Large interior areas = tatami / fill stitch, with the STITCH ANGLE ROTATED DIFFERENTLY for each adjacent block so light catches each panel differently and the block structure is clearly readable.
- Outlines, borders and all lettering = raised satin-stitch columns, 3-4mm wide, tightly packed diagonal thread passes, domed and glossy.
- Small accents, eyes and dots = dense satin blocks sitting slightly higher than the fill beneath them.
- Fine interior details = single-pass running stitch, broken and slightly wobbly, with individual needle penetration points visible.

STEP 3 — PHYSICAL REALISM:
- Embroidery sits 1.5-2mm proud of the fabric and casts a soft contact shadow.
- Visible seam line where two thread colors butt against each other.
- Ragged fringe of individual thread tips along the outer silhouette; fabric nap fibers slightly overlapping the edges.
- Strong directional specular sheen on polyester thread — some stitch rows bright, adjacent rows darker purely from thread direction.
- Slight fabric puckering and needle holes around the stitched block.

STEP 4 — MOCKUP PRESENTATION:
- Place the finished embroidery centered on the chest of a folded garment.
- Top-down flat lay on warm oak wood, soft natural window light from upper left, cozy seasonal props (ceramic mug, candle, dried leaves) softly out of focus.
- Photorealistic product photograph, shallow depth of field, warm neutral grade, sharp macro texture on the embroidery.
