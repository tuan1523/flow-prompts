A slim, body-hugging short-sleeve crop tee in the style of a Y2K baby tee: thin ribbed crew neckline, short fitted cap sleeves that hug the upper arm, the hem ending just above the navel so a small strip of bare midriff shows at the bottom of the frame. EMBROIDERY — 1:1 FAITHFUL COPY (highest priority):
Treat the attached image as the EXACT embroidery pattern. Convert ONLY the surface into real stitched thread with CLEARLY VISIBLE texture — this must read as embroidery, never as a screen print or DTG graphic:
- Lettering (serif, script and small caps alike) = raised satin-stitch columns, slightly domed and glossy, each letter built from tight parallel thread rows following the letter's stroke direction.
- Fine line-art, sketch hatching and thin outlines = crisp running stitch and narrow satin stitch lines, the thread slightly raised with tiny visible gaps between stitch segments — delicate, like hand-drawn lines rebuilt in thread.
- Any solid or filled color area = dense tatami fill stitches, with the STITCH ANGLE ROTATED DIFFERENTLY in adjacent areas so light catches each block differently.
- Soft directional sheen on the polyester/rayon thread — some stitch rows bright, neighboring rows darker purely from thread direction.
- Individual thread strands visible along edges, at letter ends and at the tips of fine lines.
Reproduce every letter, word, number and spelling EXACTLY as in the attached image — same fonts/lettering style, same icons, shapes, layout, spacing, proportions and the exact same thread colors. Do not redraw, restyle, simplify, add or drop anything. EXACT means the same shapes, layout and colors — but the SURFACE must be re-rendered as 100% stitched thread: never paste the artwork's flat pixels onto the tee, every single area is rebuilt from visible stitches. The embroidery follows the gentle curve of the chest and the stretch of the knit, but its geometry stays identical.

PHYSICAL STITCH REALISM ON THIN JERSEY:
Because the tee is thin stretchy knit, the embroidery shows a natural, gentle PULL: the fabric very slightly gathers and puckers right along the edges of the lettering and denser stitched areas, with tiny radial micro-wrinkles around them; the stitched areas sit slightly proud of the fabric and cast a soft contact shadow; tiny needle holes along stitch borders. Keep it subtle and realistic — sewn into the tee, the fabric further from the stitches stays smooth. The embroidery is slightly stiffer than the surrounding knit, so it stays flatter while the jersey drapes softly around it.

FABRIC REALISM:
Smooth fine cotton-spandex jersey with a soft matte surface — tight even knit, texture only subtly visible up close, clean and smooth, NOT fuzzy, NOT pilled. The tee hugs the body with soft natural stretch lines at the underarms and gentle folds at the waist, and gentle tonal variation in the tee color — slightly lighter where light hits, deeper in the folds — never one flat uniform block of color. LIGHT: bright soft natural daylight, neutral white balance, true-to-life colors, soft realistic shadows — with a gentle side light falling across the chest so the raised satin stitches and thread rows catch small highlights and cast tiny micro-shadows, making the embroidery pop as a real relief surface.
