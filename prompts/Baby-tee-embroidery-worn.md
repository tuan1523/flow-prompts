Close-up lifestyle photo of an EMBROIDERED baby tee: the attached design is machine-embroidered onto the center chest with flat, low-profile satin and fill stitches in shiny polyester thread — exactly like the real embroidered baby tees sold in small boutiques and Etsy shops. Up close it is clearly thread, not ink: glossy stitch rows, a slight relief and the thin knit gently gathered around the stitching.

PRIORITY ORDER (when rules compete, the higher one wins):
1. The design reads as real flat machine embroidery, never as ink on fabric.
2. Every word, letter and number is spelled exactly as in the attachment, in the same layout, arrangement and thread colors.
3. The garment is a fitted cropped baby tee.
4. Scene, pose and styling.
Only the line weight may change, and only as much as embroidery physically requires (see below); fonts, drawing, layout and composition stay as in the attachment.

THE ATTACHMENT IS ONLY A STITCH MAP: use it to know WHAT the design shows — its words, drawing, layout and colors. Ignore everything else in it: any garment, model, pose, props, lighting and the flat surface of the artwork. Rebuild every part of the design from thread; never transfer its pixels onto the tee.

EMBROIDERY — FLAT MACHINE EMBROIDERY ON A THIN TEE:
- Lettering = satin-stitch columns with tightly packed diagonal thread passes, the diagonal rows visible inside each letter. Same font style as the attachment; strokes thinner than about 1.5 mm are stitched at 1.5 mm, which is the only change allowed.
- Drawings, sketch lines and outlines = narrow satin lines about 1.5 mm wide or short running stitches, following the same paths as the original drawing; very dense hatching is stitched as fewer lines along the same directions, so the drawing keeps its silhouette and character.
- Filled color areas = tatami fill with the stitch angle differing between adjacent blocks.
- The thread has a soft satin sheen that changes with stitch direction — rows facing the light brighter, other rows a touch darker.
- Low relief: the stitching sits only about 1 mm above the knit, lying flat and following the curve of the body, with a faint contact shadow along its edges.
- The thin jersey is gently gathered by the stitching: fine small puckers and soft ripples a few millimeters around the lettering and denser areas, the kind every real embroidered thin tee has. The puckers stay in the fabric around the design; the letters and drawing themselves stay clean and undistorted.
- Tiny needle holes and a few thread tips visible along the edges when seen up close.

GARMENT — BABY TEE:
A slim, body-hugging short-sleeve crop tee in the Y2K baby tee style: thin ribbed crew neckline, short fitted sleeves hugging the upper arm, hem ending just above the navel so a small strip of bare midriff shows at the bottom of the frame. Lightweight, soft and stretchy — never boxy, never oversized, never a sweatshirt.
